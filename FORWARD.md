# Forward

4B pretrain train step. The network field is 256³ at 4 nm. Each row is the native spacing, ordered z × y × x. Read sizes are the voxel counts that cover that field: `round(256 × 4 / nm)`.

The SSL order is Read, Augment, Degrade, Corrupt, Missing. Augment on every row is one gamma for the whole crop (probability 0.5, gamma 0.8–1.25). That volume is the loss target, and it is the same along every axis. Degrade nearest-downsamples z of it only on a 4 nm crop (`zf` 2–10). Corrupt is per-section jitter (probability 0.5, ±2 voxels), per-section gamma (probability 0.5, 0.8–1.25), and added noise (probability 0.3, std 0.01), on the degraded input only. Missing runs last on every row: it zeroes whole native sections (probability 0.3, at most 2, fill zero), so each one is a whole blank slab on the 4 nm cube. After the forward pass, Resample average-pools the 4 nm raw head to the native resolution. A 4 nm crop skips it.

On train, a random zyx permutation is applied after the degraded image is on the 4 nm cube. The augmented target is permuted with it, then nearest-upsampled, and Resample uses that permuted native resolution. Batch size stays 2. Validation skips the permutation. TensorBoard shows the 256³ cube on every row. The flow term stays on the 4 nm cube.
