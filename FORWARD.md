# Forward

4B pretrain train step. The network field is 256³ at 4 nm. Each row is the native spacing, ordered z × y × x. Read sizes are the voxel counts that cover that field: `round(256 × 4 / nm)`.

Augment on every row is jitter (probability 0.5, ±2 voxels per section) then contrast (probability 0.5, gamma 0.8–1.25). That volume is the loss target. Degrade nearest-downsamples z of it only on a 4 nm crop (`zf` 2–10). Corrupt is missing sections (probability 0.3, at most 2, fill zero) and added noise (probability 0.3, std 0.01), on the degraded input only. After the forward pass, Resample average-pools the 4 nm raw head to the native resolution. A 4 nm crop skips it.

On train, a random zyx permutation is applied after the degraded image is on the 4 nm cube. The augmented target is permuted with it, then nearest-upsampled, and Resample uses that permuted native resolution. Batch size stays 2. Validation skips the permutation. TensorBoard shows the 256³ cube on every row. The flow term stays on the 4 nm cube.
